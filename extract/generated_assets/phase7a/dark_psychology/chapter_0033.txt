Chapter 8: Powerful Verbal and Nonverbal Cues
Verbal communication encompasses both spoken and written words. As opposed to nonverbal communication which requires active thought. This form of communication has no words or sounds to rely on. Nothing but what you see with your eyes and what you make of it. When we use gestures, body movements, and facial expressions to convey our intent that is a form of nonverbal communication.
What both forms of communication have is that they matter. They’re equally important contributors to the overall communication process. Your posture, facial expression, gestures that you make are going to be the clues that your employer is looking for when they assess you.
